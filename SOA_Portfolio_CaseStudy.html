D The finding that matters

Assessment

The data is clean; the exposure is operational.

Every one of the 155 line items sits at Step 7 of 12 — "Emailed SOA to PMO." Nothing downstream is recorded as approved, PAF'd, or paid, so the full $39,949.27 is outstanding, with 104 of 155 invoices still pending separation.

Because the queue is single-threaded through one approval hand-off while the monthly run-rate climbs, the backlog lengthens faster than it clears. The constraint is the Step 7→8→9 hand-off, not order volume — which means the fix is process instrumentation, not more data.

E What I built

01 · Reconcile

One dataset from many sources

Parsed 14 monthly workbooks and the cost tracker into a single 155-row register, cross-checked to the cent against the A&L publication and flagged a PMO naming split that fragments un-normalized rollups.

02 · Rebuild

A tracker that can't drift

Replaced a flat, hand-summed tab with a line-item register feeding read-only SUMIFS/COUNTIFS views, a normalized site map, dropdown validation, and a workflow funnel with an aging clock — 835 formulas, zero errors.

03 · Recommend

Make the backlog reportable

Laid out a SharePoint-list → Power Automate → Power BI path to timestamp each hand-off, and specific A&L slide changes so leadership sees throughput and trend, not just order counts.
